# Art Book wallpapers

These images are composed for Zlyme from Art Book Next.

Source: https://github.com/anthonycaccese/art-book-next-es-de
Commit: `d772d07109701d9bd7c9fda305bfef6601105ab8`
Author: Anthony Caccese

The system pictures use `_inc/systems/artwork` and `_inc/systems/logos`. They are 640×480 RGBA with a transparent background. The logo is at most 291 pixels wide, including a 2px black contour and a 16px black shadow at 50% opacity, and it stays in the right-hand half of the frame. There is no bundled ROM-list wallpaper.
